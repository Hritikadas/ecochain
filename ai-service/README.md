# 🌱 EcoChain AI Carbon Coach

An AI-powered sustainability assistant that helps you understand and reduce your carbon footprint.

## Features
- 💬 Chat with the AI Carbon Coach about sustainability topics
- 📊 Calculate your carbon footprint based on commute, diet, and electricity usage
- 🌳 Get personalized tips to reduce your environmental impact
- 🔗 Connected to the EcoChain blockchain carbon tracking platform

## How to Use
1. **Chat Tab**: Ask any question about carbon emissions, sustainability, or eco-friendly practices
2. **Calculator Tab**: Enter your daily habits to calculate your carbon footprint

## Tech Stack
- FastAPI + Gradio for the web interface
- RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) with EPA climate knowledge base
- Deployed on HuggingFace Spaces
